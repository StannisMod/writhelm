# Retrospective findings and corrective measures

Contract K-RETRO, revision 1. Status: eight disclosed decisions approved by owner; not implemented
or effectiveness-qualified. Governing [K-HARNESS](agent-workflow.md), [K-TASK](task-contract.md),
owner normative authority, fact/evidence and immutable scoped-knowledge laws remain unchanged.

## RT-01 — Attributed retrospective, not automatic SOP creation

Retrospective accompanies result handoff/completion and substantive failures/blockers. Bind task
revision/assignment/result and evidence; it may be part of existing report. No mandatory additional
agent/model call. An explicit no-significant-new-findings outcome is valid; don't fabricate a SOP.
Not every ordinary tool action requires a new record.

## RT-02 — Separate observation, expectation, cause and recommendation

Distinguish observed behavior/evidence, applicable prior requirement or newly expressed expectation,
consequence, cause hypothesis and uncertainty. A new expectation is not an invented historical rule.
Worker report/hypothesis isn't verified fact, owner approval or authority to change task/design.

## RT-03 — Diagnose before choosing a measure

Check absent/misdelivered/conflicting existing rule, missing input, unclear objective, reasoning error
and unsafe tool/interface boundary. Find applicable existing rules before adding another. A new
failure/regression test doesn't automatically justify a new norm. Confirmed violations of existing
requirements remain blocking where applicable regardless of SOP proposal status.

## RT-04 — Fix at the appropriate boundary

Measures include task input/structured factual memory, owner clarification, enforcing/delivering an
existing rule, correcting type/API/validator/permission boundary, proposing SOP, or retaining a case/
open question without general rule. Routine corrections within approved task/design can proceed
under existing authority; retrospective doesn't grant new scope or rewrite requirements.

## RT-05 — Scoped SOP proposals and counterexamples

Candidate identifies recognizable applicability/trigger, required behavior, rationale/risk, exclusions
and existing norms replaced/refined. Generalize over the cause, not incident wording; universality
isn't required. Where feasible check original case, materially different same-cause case and similar
case not needing intervention. One severe/foreseeable risk may justify precautionary proposal;
fixed recurrence counts or forced generalization are not required. No useful general rule is a valid
outcome, not an automatic project block or invented norm.

## RT-06 — Owner decides new prescriptions

Agent proposes analysis, options, costs and limits. Owner approves/rejects/requests changes to new/
changed prescriptions through exact decision mechanism. Approval does not prove cause/efficacy.
No repeated owner confirmation is needed for an already-authorized routine implementation fix.
Proposed rules stay labelled, cannot silently enter mandatory context. Open questions transfer
with responsibility rather than disappear when the producing worker finishes.

## RT-07 — Norm lifecycle, applicability and effectiveness

Approved norm has exact revision/scope/provenance, duplicate replacement/refinement and an effect-
check plan/status plus reconsideration/retirement conditions. Unmeasured efficacy stays visible;
adoption doesn't authorize live/paid tests. Authorized retirement preserves history. Ordinary update
doesn't silently rebind active work; urgent withdrawal follows explicit blocking/rebind rules.

## RT-08 — Hook enforces procedure, not diagnosis/SOP count

Hook/service handoff checks required attributed retrospective or explicit no-new-findings outcome.
It does not prove semantic/causal completeness, autoapprove norms, force SOP per incident or a new
LLM call per checkbox. Missing required report part may block handoff, not erase artifacts. No SOP
for a situation does not itself forbid ordinary authorized work under task/authority/general rules.

## Readiness and approval provenance

Pin schemas, rights, task/report bindings, native hook behavior and finite checks before implementation.
Separate observation/proposal/accepted prescription/effect evidence; do not import private project
SOPs, incidents or raw sessions into public source. Owner explicitly approved K-RETRO v1 and
K-CONTEXT v2 separately named in the proposal with “утверждаю оба” on 2026-11-05. This manual record
is not implemented automation, a factual efficacy proof, live-call permission or commit/push approval.
