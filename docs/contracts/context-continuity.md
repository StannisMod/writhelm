# Task-shaped context, extraction and native compaction

Contract K-CONTEXT, revision 4. Status: task-shaped decisions, practical-control and minimal connected-work delivery amendments approved;

compaction/extraction implementation or qualification claimed. Replaces proposed draft1. Governing
[K-HARNESS](agent-workflow.md), [K-TASK](task-contract.md), [K-DISPATCH](dispatch.md),

## CT-01 — Disposable context, recoverable operational knowledge

Information needed to continue/justify work must not exist only in conversation. Tasks, reports and
linked structured memory retain it or accessible exact source/artifact references. Real code/files/
systems/processes still exist and must be reconciled against records. Don't copy whole corpora or
transcripts into canonical memory or destroy audit artifacts just to clear active context. Secrets
remain outside model/task-memory/extraction in protected credential mechanisms.

## CT-02 — Restore exact obligations

Base includes exact task revision/assignment, valid selected snapshot, declared applicable SOP/norm
closure, inputs/scope/rights and profile. Service checks declared mandatory closure from explicit
applicability/task inputs, not worker convenience. This doesn't prove planner discovered every
semantically applicable norm. Manifest records original versions/clauses/inclusion reasons.
Mandatory source text isn't replaced by paraphrase. Missing/denied/withdrawn/ambiguous/conflicting/
oversized inputs refuse/escalate, not omit/latest-fallback. Older valid snapshots allowed. Structural
closure isn't semantic completeness; optional examples/retrieval remain explicitly non-normative.

Fixed input base differs from progressively loaded view. Worker may inspect more files/material and
read required SOPs from the same pinned base without task/rebind solely for that read. Keep exact
references and record new material without rewriting original manifest or pretending its former
closure certified all undiscovered applicability. Handle new conflict/gap before affected use under
current rules. Changed base/revision, goal, requirements or authority is not ordinary loading and
still needs explicit decision/rebind as applicable.

## CT-03 — Target extraction toward remaining obligations

Extract progress/next steps, needed findings/reasons/negative results/hypotheses/uncertainty, blockers,
awaited decisions/events and unfinished operations. Relevance is to ALL remaining obligations,
including useful findings from completed steps. Preserve source-backed facts/necessary exact fragments,
original references/status/evidence applicability, not a chronology. A valid schema/model selection
doesn't prove semantic completeness; known gaps stay explicit.

## CT-04 — Retrospective is not hidden instruction promotion

Carry relevant observations, known defects, proposed measures and open questions with source task/
report/evidence/status. Only authorized accepted prescriptions enter mandatory norms under snapshot/
update/withdrawal laws. Owner assent doesn't prove factual cause/efficacy. Proposed SOP is not an
obligation; confirmed violation of an existing requirement still blocks affected work. Repeated
extraction doesn't promote a hypothesis/case memory to established fact or binding norm.

## CT-05 — Finished versus unfinished executor context

After durable complete handoff of exact result/report/retrospective/open obligations and effects,
finished executor may discard active task context without waiting for downstream result acceptance.
Main-session user report/notification must be recoverably delivered, not physically read by the user.
Owner questions transfer explicitly. Later remediation is a new bound task/result, not endless old
worker history. Context retirement alone doesn't prove tool completion or release write claims.

Unfinished work restores exact base, tracker, targeted residual knowledge, artifact/workspace identity,
blockers/events/outstanding operations. Planner includes whole active task/dependency/decision
frontier, not merely last topic. Neither outcome may discard unique necessary operational knowledge.

## CT-06 — Persist and confirm before replacing context

Bind source/assignment/checkpoint basis and persist/confirm continuation state before retiring old
active context. Events after the cut remain pending for delivery/reconciliation. Failed/knowingly
incomplete extraction retains old context or blocks, never silently continues with losses. Lost
acknowledgement is reconciled, not retried as new task/command. No cross-system atomicity promise
between Git and OpenCode. Restart reconciles actual work/effects; checkpoint isn't verification.

## CT-07 — Native lifecycle, task-shaped retained state

Pin effective per-worker model/route/effort/context/output reserves and native compaction policy
independently of planner defaults. Use qualified native threshold/scheduling/lifecycle with reserve;
retained content follows this contract, not arbitrary narrative summary. Prefer supported task-aware
compaction/instruction mechanisms; minimal adapter only for demonstrated gap, no second independent
compaction trigger/counter by default. If mandatory/continuation packet can't fit, refuse/replan rather
than compress obligations into new meaning. Trigger isn't proven hard ceiling; no silent downgrade.
Model-based extraction/live-route qualification requires separate authorization, not per-call human
approval/Git journaling or unapproved model experiment from contract acceptance.

## CT-08 — Recoverability and visible limits

Expose exact task/profile/snapshot/continuation packet, progress, factual/proposed/normative statuses,
gaps/pending operations/deferred checks. Qualify finished/unfinished handoffs, significant completed-
step discovery needed later, lost reply/late event, proposed-SOP non-promotion, changed artifacts/stale
evidence, omitted mandatory-input refusal and planner multi-task frontier. Finite evidence doesn't
prove lossless semantic extraction for arbitrary text. Ordinary updates notify without rebind;
withdrawal blocks affected use at qualified boundary. Unsupported native/accounting/OS profiles
stay unqualified, not prompt-only guarantees or permissive fallback.

## CT-09 — Minimal sufficient continuation notification

An ordinary resolution event tells the affected executor "blocker B resolved, continue". Do not
repeat known task/norms, repair history, full diff or mechanical identity details by default. Supply
additional explanation only when necessary for the next action; do not hide relevant semantic changes
or omit known required information merely to shorten a message. Exact supporting material remains
available through authorized references, not necessarily loaded into every consumer's context.

Exact snapshots underpin reproducible access and validation, not a requirement to duplicate knowledge
in agent conversations. Engine-prepared environments and canonical records, rather than planner
retelling, establish what is available and confirmed. Notification alone cannot change authority,
assigned bases or an agreed interface/behavior. Retain pending delivery across loss/restart under CT-06.
